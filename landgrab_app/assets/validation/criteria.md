# What to check

You're confirming each pole is **real, correctly placed, and safe to
send players to.** Replace this file (`assets/validation/criteria.md`)
with the real criteria — it's plain Markdown.

## At the pole
- **Scan the barcode** — it should match what's on file.
- Check the pin sits where the pole actually is; drag it to correct it.
- Note anything that affects access (stairs, locked after hours, etc.).

## Flag it if
- The barcode doesn't match, or there's no pole here.
- The recorded spot is off by more than a few metres.
- It's unsafe or hard to reach.
